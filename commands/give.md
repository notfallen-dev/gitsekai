# /give

## How to use

First of all you type `/give jewel 500000` (jewel AKA crystals). It gives you 500K gems! But you can use ID too! IDs are most of all used for certain materials. For example `/give material 10 100` will give 100 of Mysterious Gems! Now to list of all materials that you can get without ID and with ID!

## No ID

`jewel`, `paid_jewel`, `coin`, `virtual_coin`, `live_point`&#x20;
